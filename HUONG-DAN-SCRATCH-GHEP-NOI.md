## Cach dua vao project Scratch cua ban

5. Bam **Share** neu can nop bai

## Cach choi
1. Man **Mo dau** → bam sprite **BtnBatDau**
2. **Cap 1**: keo the tieng Anh (EN) den the nghia Viet (VI) dung cap → 5 cap
3. Sau cap 1 → bam **BtnCapTiep** (Cap 2 nhanh hon)
4. Het cap 2 → man **Ket thuc**

## Noi dung giao duc
- Cap 1: Apple-Tao, Cat-Meo, Book-Sach, Sun-Mat troi, Water-Nuoc
- Cap 2: Elephant-Voi, Computer-May tinh, School-Truong hoc, Friend-Ban be, Happy-Vui ve

## Da co trong project (theo de bai)
- 5 cap tu moi cap (10 the)
- Di chuyen ngau nhien khi chua ghep
- Keo tha chuot de ghep
- Ghep dung: dinh nhau, di chuyen cung nhau
- Ghep sai: bao "Chua dung!" + hieu ung mau
- Khối tu tao: `phat hieu ung sai` (moi the)
- 3 man: Mo dau, Choi (Cap 1/2), Ket thuc
- 2 cap do kho (toc do 4 vs 8/10)
- 2 nut: BtnBatDau, BtnCapTiep
- Bien: soCapDaGhep, capDo
- Danh sach: danhSachCap1, danhSachCap2
- Tin nhan: batDau, batCap1, batCap2, ghepDung, ghepSai, thangCuoc...
